# Customer Guide

## Browse and Search

- Use `/shop` for all products.
- Use category filters and product pages for details.
- Use search to find specific products quickly.

## Place an Order

1. Add products to cart.
2. Open `/checkout`.
3. Choose payment method.
4. Confirm order.

## Payment Methods

- Card (Stripe)
- Paystack-supported options
- Mobile money in supported regions
- Cash on delivery when enabled

## Track Orders

- Open your account dashboard.
- Go to orders list.
- Open an order to view status and details.

## Book Salon Services

1. Browse salons and services.
2. Select date and slot.
3. Confirm booking.
4. Track booking status in your account.

## Loyalty and Referrals

- Earn points from eligible purchases.
- Use referral features where enabled.

## Account Security Tips

- Use a strong password.
- Keep your email verified and current.
- Sign out on shared devices.
