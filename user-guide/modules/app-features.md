Here’s a **plan-tiered breakdown of the premium modules available in the **Smart African Market** ecosystem** — grouped by the subscription tiers where they’re offered (based on official module listings and features pricing info).

---

## 📌 **Starter Plan Modules**
Includes a core set of essential modules to get your marketplace up and running.
Includes a small set of core premium modules ideal for basic marketplace needs:

* **Vendor Support**
* **EU Compliance Fields**
* **Delivery Time**
* **Color Scheme Customizer**

*These are the modules shown as available starting even from the Starter tier.* 

---

## 💼 **Professional Plan Modules**

Everything in Starter **plus** additional advanced modules:

* **Paystack Module**
* **Seller Badge**
* **Stripe Express**
* **Report Abuse**
* **Stripe Connect**
* **Table Rate Shipping**
* **Minimum / Maximum Order**
* **Subscriptions**
* **Follow Store**
* **Product Addon**
* **Ajax Live Search**
* **Return and Warranty Request**
* **Seller Verification**
* **Single Product Multivendor**
* **Store Support**
* **Vendor Review**
  *(Plus all Starter modules)* 

> This tier typically includes around ~23 premium modules.

---

## 🏆 **Business Plan Modules**

Everything in Professional **plus** more feature-rich modules:

* **Request for Quotation**
* **Product Advertising**
* **Geolocation Search**
* **Export Import**
* **Live Chat**
* **Product Enquiry**
* **ShipStation Integration**
* **Vendor Staff Manager**
* **Vendor Vacation**
* **Wholesale / B2B Module**
* **Vendor Analytics**
* **Product Q&A**
* **Product Subscription**
  *(All Professional + Starter modules, too)* 

> Business plan generally gives access to 42+ modules.

---

## 💼 **Enterprise Plan Modules**

Includes **everything from Business** **plus:**

* **Priority support** and **services** (e.g., 1-hour theme compatibility/installation support)
* Full access to all Smart African Market modules included in Business
  *(There are no additional modules beyond Business — the Enterprise differences are support and site subscriptions, not extra add-ons.)* 

---

## 🗂 **Free / Separate Smart African Market Add-ons**

These are not part of the module toggles in the Dokan Pro Modules screen; instead, they are separate plugins you install:

* **App Migrator**
* **App PDF Invoice**
* **App Product Duplicator**
* **App Vendor Dashboard**
  *(These are free or separate from the main Pro module list.)*

---

### 🛠 Notes

* Modules like **Service Bookings Integration** and **Auctions Addons** are available as standalone add-ons and bundled into Business & Enterprise plans — but they install separately. 
* Exact availability can change with Dokan updates and new releases.
