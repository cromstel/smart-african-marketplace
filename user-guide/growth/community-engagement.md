# Community Engagement Playbook

This playbook governs community-building activities around the marketplace.

## Engagement Channels
- Social comments and community threads
- Vendor community sessions/webinars
- Customer feedback roundtables

## Monthly Operating Cycle
1. Define community topics and goals.
2. Schedule events/posts and assign moderators.
3. Capture insights, questions, and recurring pain points.
4. Route actionable feedback to product/support owners.
5. Publish follow-up updates to the community.

## Moderation Standards
- Respectful, inclusive communication
- Remove abusive or spam content
- Escalate sensitive issues to support/legal as needed

## KPIs
- Active community participants
- Response time in community channels
- Feedback items converted to product actions
