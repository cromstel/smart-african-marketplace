# Influencer Partnerships Program

This program defines how influencer collaborations are selected, executed, and measured.

## Partner Selection Criteria
- Audience relevance to target customer segments
- Authentic engagement quality over follower volume
- Brand-safety and content quality history

## Campaign Workflow
1. Define objective, audience, budget, and KPI.
2. Shortlist partners and complete due diligence.
3. Issue collaboration brief with deliverables and timeline.
4. Publish using tracked links/codes.
5. Review performance and renewal decisions.

## Required Controls
- Written agreement with disclosure requirements
- Content approval and compliance check before publish
- Fraud check on traffic/conversions

## KPIs
- Attributed revenue
- Cost per acquisition
- Engagement-to-conversion rate
