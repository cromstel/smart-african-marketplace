# Payment Compliance Audit Runbook

This runbook defines recurring payment compliance checks.

## Scope
- Checkout and payment capture flows
- Webhook validation and reconciliation
- Refund and dispute handling controls
- Access controls for payment operations

## Audit Cadence
- Quarterly internal audit
- Post-incident audit after major payment incidents

## Checklist
- Verify secret key handling and rotation procedures.
- Verify webhook signature validation remains enforced.
- Verify reconciliation reports match processor records.
- Verify refund/dispute workflows and approvals.
- Verify payment-related logs and access trails are retained.

## Deliverables
- Audit report with pass/fail by control
- Remediation plan for failed controls
- Follow-up validation after fixes
