# Regional Compliance Expansion Runbook

This runbook defines compliance readiness for entering new geographic markets.

## Scope Per New Market
- Business registration and marketplace licensing requirements
- Consumer protection and refund disclosure obligations
- Data protection/privacy obligations and cross-border transfer rules
- Tax registration, invoicing, and reporting requirements
- Payment method and settlement compliance constraints

## Expansion Workflow
1. Identify target market and regulatory owner.
2. Build market compliance checklist with legal/finance/ops input.
3. Gap-assess current platform policies and operations.
4. Implement required policy/process updates before launch.
5. Validate support, payout, and tax handling with test scenarios.
6. Approve go-live with documented sign-off.

## Ongoing Controls
- Quarterly review for regulatory changes in each active market.
- Incident escalation path for non-compliance findings.
- Maintain evidence of approvals and policy effective dates.
