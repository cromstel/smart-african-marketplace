# Terms and Policies Review Cadence

This cadence ensures legal and policy pages stay aligned with product and regulatory changes.

## Scope
- Terms of Service
- Privacy Policy
- Refund and return policy references
- Vendor policy clauses and compliance notices

## Review Frequency
- Quarterly scheduled review
- Immediate review after major feature, payment, or data-handling changes
- Immediate review when legal/regulatory updates apply

## Review Workflow
1. Collect product and operations changes since last review.
2. Compare current policy text against operational reality.
3. Submit legal/content updates for approval.
4. Publish updates with effective date.
5. Notify support and operations teams of changes.

## Evidence
- Review date and owner
- Change summary
- Approval record
- Published effective date
