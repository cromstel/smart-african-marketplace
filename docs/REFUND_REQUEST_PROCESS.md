# Refund Request Process

This process defines refund intake, validation, approval, and closure.

## Eligibility Intake
- Confirm order ID, item(s), and request reason.
- Check return/refund window and product eligibility.
- Verify delivery status and vendor policy constraints.

## Processing Flow
1. Create refund case with timestamp and owner.
2. Validate evidence (photos, damaged/missing proof when required).
3. Determine decision: approve, partial approve, or reject with reason.
4. Execute refund via original payment channel where possible.
5. Notify customer and vendor with outcome.

## SLAs
- Initial assessment within 48 hours.
- Final decision within 5 business days.
- Payment reversal timing follows processor SLAs.

## Controls
- Track refund reason codes for trend analysis.
- Escalate high-value or repeated abuse patterns.
