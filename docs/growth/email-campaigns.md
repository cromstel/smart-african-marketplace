# Email Newsletter Campaigns Playbook

This playbook governs recurring email campaign execution.

## Cadence
- Weekly newsletter baseline
- Additional sends for seasonal campaigns and major launches

## Audience Segments
- Customers (active, dormant, high-value)
- Vendors (onboarding, growth, retention)
- Subscribers by category interests

## Workflow
1. Define campaign objective and segment.
2. Build content block with CTA and tracking links.
3. Run QA (links, personalization, unsubscribe, rendering).
4. Schedule send and monitor delivery metrics.
5. Publish post-send performance report.

## KPIs
- Open rate
- Click-through rate
- Conversion rate
- Unsubscribe rate
