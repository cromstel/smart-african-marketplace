# Product Catalog Refresh Schedule

This schedule defines how marketplace inventory stays current and relevant.

## Weekly Cadence
- Review low-stock and out-of-stock catalog segments.
- Identify stale listings with no updates in 30+ days.
- Request vendors to refresh pricing, availability, and images.

## Monthly Cadence
- Curate and publish featured new arrivals by category.
- Archive or unlist non-compliant/stale products per policy.
- Audit top categories for assortment gaps and vendor diversity.

## KPIs
- New products published per week
- % stale listings older than 30 days
- Category coverage versus target assortment
