# Social Media Posting Calendar

This calendar defines consistent social publishing operations.

## Cadence
- Minimum 4 posts per week across active channels
- Daily story/reel cadence during active campaigns

## Content Mix
- Product highlights
- Vendor stories
- Educational/cultural content
- Campaign and promo announcements

## Workflow
1. Plan weekly content slate with owners.
2. Prepare copy/creative and approvals.
3. Publish by channel schedule.
4. Track engagement and top-performing content.

## KPIs
- Reach and engagement rate
- CTR to marketplace pages
- Conversion-assisted sessions
