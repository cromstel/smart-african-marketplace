# Seasonal Campaign Playbook

This playbook standardizes campaign planning for peak seasonal periods.

## Campaign Windows
- Q1: New year and cultural events
- Q2: Spring collections and gifting
- Q3: Back-to-school and travel
- Q4: Holiday and year-end promotions

## Execution Timeline
- T-6 weeks: define offer, goals, and channels.
- T-4 weeks: finalize creative, landing assets, and inventory alignment.
- T-2 weeks: QA links, checkout flows, and promo codes.
- Launch week: monitor conversion and fulfillment risk daily.

## Required Deliverables
- Campaign brief with KPI targets
- Homepage hero/update plan
- Email/social/onsite copy kit
- Post-campaign performance summary
