# Blog Content Calendar

This calendar framework governs marketplace editorial updates.

## Publishing Cadence
- Minimum one post per week.
- Mix content across vendor stories, buying guides, and platform updates.

## Monthly Planning
1. Build 4-week topic slate with owners and publish dates.
2. Map each post to SEO keyword cluster and target audience.
3. Include at least one vendor spotlight and one seasonal trend post.

## Quality Checklist
- Accurate links and product references
- Clear CTA to shop, book, or register as vendor
- Metadata title/description filled for SEO
