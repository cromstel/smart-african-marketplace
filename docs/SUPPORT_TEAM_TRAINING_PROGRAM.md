# Support Team Training Program

This program defines onboarding and continuous training for support agents.

## Training Cadence
- New hires: 2-week onboarding track
- Existing team: monthly refresh sessions
- Change-based: release briefing for major workflow updates

## Core Modules
- Account, order, refund, and vendor support flows
- Escalation procedures and SLA handling
- Policy interpretation boundaries and legal escalation
- Communication quality and de-escalation techniques

## Certification
- Scenario-based assessment after onboarding
- Quarterly quality review with coaching actions

## KPIs
- First response time
- Resolution time
- Customer satisfaction score
- Reopen rate
