# Review Moderation Policy

This policy defines consistent moderation standards for marketplace reviews.

## Allowed Content
- Honest experience with purchased product/service
- Constructive feedback relevant to listing quality

## Prohibited Content
- Hate speech, harassment, threats
- Personal data disclosure
- Spam, fake promotion, or irrelevant links
- Defamatory or clearly fabricated claims

## Moderation Workflow
1. Flag review manually or via report signal.
2. Validate purchase/context and policy violation.
3. Decide action: keep, edit/redact (if supported), hide/remove.
4. Record moderation reason and moderator identity.
5. Notify user when action requires explanation.

## SLA
- High-risk/abusive reports: same-day action
- Standard reports: action within 3 business days

## Audit
- Monthly moderation sample review for consistency.
