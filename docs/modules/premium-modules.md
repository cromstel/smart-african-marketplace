Premium Modules
===============
Develop these premium modules if not already implemented and must be subscription RBAC.

1. Vendor Support Module : 
   The Vendor Support Module makes communication between vendors and admins easy.
   Available in : Professional, Business, Enterprise
   
2. Subscriptions : 
   Create highly customized subscription packs and charge vendors.
   Available in : Professional, Business, Enterpriseprocee
   
3. Seller Badge : 
   Acknowledge Vendors for their Marketplace Accomplishments with Badges
   Available in : Professional, Business, Enterprise

4. Stripe Express : 
   Transaction Made Effortless with Dynamic Payment Options
   Available in : Professional, Business, Enterprise

5. Request for Quotation : 
   Enable Price Quote Option on the Marketplace
   Available in : Business, Enterprise

6. Report Abuse : 
   Maintain A Reliable Marketplace Where Customers Are Prioritize
   Available in : Professional, Business, Enterprise

7. Follow Store : 
   Following favourite vendor store is now made easier than ever with the system
   Available in : Professional, Business, Enterprise

8. Geolocation : 
   All the customers search for products / vendors by location
   Available in : Business, Enterprise

9. EU Compliance Fields : 
   Provide the fields required by EU law for both customers and vendors with TAX ID
   Available in : Professional, Business, Enterprise
   
10. Ajax Live Search : 
    Turn the regular search bar into an instant advanced product searching tool.
	Available in : Starter, Professional, Business, Enterprise

11. Color Scheme Customizer : 
    Change the color of vendor dashboard to match with third-party themes.
	Available in : Starter, Professional, Business, Enterprise

12. Export Import
    Allow vendors to export & import their products & details in .xml and .csv files
    Available in : Business, Enterprise

13. Live Chat
    For vendors to give quick real-time support to registered users & customers.
	Available in : Business, Enterprise
	
14. Product Enquiry
    Let visitors directly contact vendor about the product from a product page.
	Available in : Business, Enterprise
	
15. Return and Warranty Request
    This module enables the admin to allow vendors to offer customized return
	Available in : Professional, Business, Enterprise
	
16. Seller Verification
    Check the authenticity of vendors via their social profiles, address, phone & photo.
    Available in : Professional, Business, Enterprise
	
17. Store Support
    Get all the customer support features at the convenience of the frontend.
	Available in : Professional, Business, Enterprise
	
18. Vendor Review
    Enable customers to rate & post reviews in any store page on the website
	Available in : Professional, Business, Enterprise

19. Vendor Staff Manager
    Let the sellers appoint unlimited staffs to manage their online stores from their dashboard
    Available in : Business, Enterprise

20. Wholesale
    Allows the vendors to sell products in wholesale to the customers.
    Available in : Business, Enterprise

21. Vendor Analytics
    The vendors can easily see the analytics for their stores right from the frontend.
	Available in : Business, Enterprise
	
22. Product Q&A
    Allow Customers and visitors to ask questions directly to the vendor.

23. Paystack Module
	Integrate Paystack, a leading payment solution in Africa, into your marketplace for a seamless & secure payment experience.
	Available in : Professional, Business, Enterprise

24. AI Product Content Generator
    Automatically generate professional & engaging product titles and description content using product information.
    Available in : Business, Enterprise
