# Getting Started

This guide helps you begin using Smart African Marketplace quickly.

## Account Types

- Customer: Buy products, book services, track orders.
- Vendor: List products, manage orders, customize storefront.
- Admin: Manage users, products, orders, subscriptions, and platform settings.

## Customer First Steps

1. Register an account from `/register`.
2. Verify your email if prompted.
3. Browse marketplace at `/shop`.
4. Add products to cart and complete checkout.
5. Track orders from your dashboard.

## Vendor First Steps

1. Register an account.
2. Request or receive vendor role access.
3. Open Vendor Dashboard at `/vendor/dashboard`.
4. Add products and pricing.
5. Configure store theme.
6. Share your store URL.

## Admin First Steps

1. Sign in at `/admin/login`.
2. Review dashboard and key alerts.
3. Confirm payment gateways are configured.
4. Review vendor approvals and product catalog.
5. Verify subscription plans and module access settings.

## Important URLs

- Home: `/`
- Shop: `/shop`
- Login: `/login`
- Admin Login: `/admin/login`
- Vendor Dashboard: `/vendor/dashboard`
- Pricing: `/pricing`
