# Paid Advertising Campaigns Runbook

This runbook defines minimum controls for paid acquisition campaigns.

## Channels
- Search ads
- Social ads
- Retargeting campaigns

## Campaign Lifecycle
1. Define objective, target audience, budget, and KPI.
2. Build creatives and landing page alignment.
3. Launch with tracking parameters and conversion events.
4. Monitor spend, CPA, and conversion quality daily.
5. Optimize bids/creatives weekly.

## Governance
- Budget caps required per campaign.
- Negative keyword/audience exclusions maintained.
- Fraud/anomaly checks for abrupt spend spikes.

## KPIs
- Cost per acquisition (CPA)
- Return on ad spend (ROAS)
- Qualified conversion volume
