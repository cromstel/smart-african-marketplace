# Tax Regulation Update Process

This process governs tax-rule monitoring and operational updates.

## Monitoring Sources
- Official tax authority publications
- Licensed tax advisor updates
- Payment processor tax/compliance notices

## Update Workflow
1. Detect relevant jurisdiction or threshold changes.
2. Assess product/checkout/reporting impact.
3. Prepare required configuration/process updates.
4. Validate in staging with sample transactions.
5. Deploy and notify finance/support teams.

## Controls
- Keep change log with effective date and owner.
- Require finance/legal approval for tax-impacting changes.
- Validate customer-facing tax messaging after updates.

## Cadence
- Monthly monitoring review
- Immediate action for urgent legal changes
